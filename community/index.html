What membership includes

A private Slack community

Meet experienced women across sectors and regions. Introduce yourself, ask for input and contribute your expertise through channels for questions and requests, events, professional opportunities, useful articles and shared resources.

Monthly community calls

A one-hour online conversation each month to exchange ideas, explore practical questions and get to know other members. Topics respond to the community's interests, with expert guests joining selected conversations.

Relevant peers and useful exchange

Connect with women bringing substantial leadership experience to advisory work, board service, fractional roles, entrepreneurship and other forms of meaningful work. Share what you know and ask for help where another perspective would be useful.

Selected partner benefits

Access selected benefits from partner organizations. We share current offers and participation details with members.

Who it is for

The Online Community is for experienced senior women who want an ongoing professional home as they work more independently. You may be preparing to leave a full-time role, building a new mix of work or strengthening an established portfolio career.

Members bring significant leadership experience and a willingness to contribute perspective, connections and useful information to others.

How to join

  1. Complete your monthly subscription through our secure checkout.
  2. Look for your welcome email with an invitation to the private Slack community and information about community calls.
  3. Introduce yourself and begin connecting with other members.

Questions before joining

Do I need to join a Peer Circle?

No. You can join the Online Community independently. Peer Circle membership includes Online Community access, so Circle members do not need a separate subscription.

How is this different from a Peer Circle?

The Online Community connects you with the broader membership through Slack and monthly community calls. Peer Circles bring a smaller, consistent group together for facilitated sessions focused on members' decisions, opportunities and challenges.

Can I join from anywhere in Canada?

Yes. The Online Community is online and welcomes experienced senior women from across Canada.

Does membership include paid work or board placement?

Members can share opportunities and make introductions. Paid engagements and board appointments are not guaranteed. The Lattice's marketplace is a future development.

What if I have a question before subscribing?

Contact hello@thelattice.ca and we will help you determine whether the community is a fit.

Looking for a consistent small group?

Explore Peer Circles for facilitated monthly sessions with the same group of experienced peers. Online Community membership is included.
