Why Now

Women aren't stepping back. They're stepping differently.

Senior women are building advisory practices, expanding board work, launching companies, taking fractional roles, and assembling portfolios that reflect the full range of what they know.

The Lattice connects them in a vetted community where experienced women leaders learn from one another, exchange referrals and opportunities, and extend one another's reach.

Who It's For

Participation is intentional.

The Lattice is for senior women leaders with at least 15 years of senior leadership experience across companies, entrepreneurial ventures, boards, professional services, the public sector, or institutions. They are:

  1. Seeking a professional home base with access to resources, connections, and tools
  2. Moving out of a traditional full-time leadership structure — or having already done so
  3. Building advisory, fractional, board, entrepreneurial, or portfolio work
  4. Looking for peers who understand both the possibilities and practical realities of working differently
  5. Ready to contribute experience, perspective, relationships, and opportunities to the strength of the network

What connects us: A deliberate choice to work on our own terms and to build whatever comes next.

Who We Are

The founding team

We built The Lattice while building our own portfolio careers.

Along the way, we heard from Canadian women seeking the same three things: community, impact, and continued income.

So we created a place designed around exactly that.
